How this works

All the apartments together own 80.048% of the building. The super's apartment (1T, 0.384%) doesn't pay common charges, so the apartments that pay add up to 79.664%.

Your share is your percentage divided by 79.664%. Then your share is multiplied by the budget. Apartments with the same percentage always pay the same amount.

Your actual bill may be off by a few cents because of rounding.
